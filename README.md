# Oxidizing

Are you tired of waiting ages for your copper blocks to oxidize so you can finally finish that weathered looking roof on your barn ?

**No longer !** This mod makes all copper blocks oxidize much faster when touching water !
The more of its six sides touch water, the faster it ages.

- Waterlogged copper counts as fully surrounded.
- Dry and waxed copper behave as in vanilla.

With one wet side, copper reaches its next stage in about 7 minutes; fully wet, in about a minute (vanilla: 20 to 27 minutes).

Have fun designing farms to take advantage of that new mechanic !

## Config

`config/oxidizing.json`: `chancePerWetSide` (default `0.1667`) is the chance per random tick for each wet side to advance one stage.
